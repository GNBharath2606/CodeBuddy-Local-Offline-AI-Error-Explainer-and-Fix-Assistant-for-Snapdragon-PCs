How it works

From red stack trace to green build in four steps.

Capture

A watcher picks up errors from your terminal, build output, or IDE diagnostic stream automatically.

Understand

A 4-bit quantized coding LLM (Qwen2.5-Coder) reasons over the error plus surrounding file context.

Explain

Plain-language diagnosis in the bottom panel — root cause, not just the top line of the trace.

Fix

A diff suggestion you can preview and apply with one click, or copy into your editor.
